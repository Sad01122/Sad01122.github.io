The Hujra Story

Built for the ones
who drive out for it

A hujra is where people gather — for tea, for talk, for a plate shared with friends. That's the idea behind ours: a roadside spot on the Trabela-Hazro Road where the tandoor stays lit and the tea stays hot, night after night.

We're known for our tikka karahi, slow-grilled BBQ, and a menu that also covers the fast-food cravings — burgers, pizza, wings and fries — so there's something for every table, from a solo biker stop to the whole family.

Outdoor lawn seating with a view of the motorway, covered dine-in for families, and a separate space that keeps things comfortable for everyone.
