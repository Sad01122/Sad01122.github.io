🧠
The name IS the formula: past + perfect + continuous = had + been + -ing → had been working.
Time changes the helping verb only: present = (base)/has-have · past = had/was-were · future = will.
Aspects never change: simple = fact · continuous = be+ing · perfect = have+V3 · PC = have been+ing.

🗺️ Pick-the-Tense Flowchart (read the signal word first!)

Two questions → any of the 12 tenses
🔑
Signal-word cheat sheet (fastest exam method — read the time word first): every day/usually/always → pres. simple · now/at the moment → pres. cont. · just/already/yet/ever/never/since/for (still true) → pres. perfect · yesterday/last…/ago/in 2019 → past simple · when/while + past → past cont. · by the time/before/after + two pasts → past perfect · tomorrow/next…/soon → will · by + future deadline → future perfect · for/since + how long → …perfect continuous.
⚠️
since vs for · in vs since: since + starting point (since Monday, since 2020) · for + duration (for five years). ❌ since 5 years ✅ for 5 years.
We worked here in 2019 (over, finished) vs We've worked here since 2019 (still true) — one small word flips the whole tense!

🔄 Flip Cards — 12 Tenses + 6 PoS (tap to flip)

Tenses

T1

Every morning, she ___ tea. (drink)

↻ tap
A

drinks — habit + she → present simple (+s).

T2

Listen! Someone ___ the door. (knock)

↻ tap
A

is knocking — Listen! = happening now → present continuous.

T3

I ___ never ___ to Paris. (be)

↻ tap
A

have never been — life experience (ever/never) → present perfect; V3 of be = been.

T4

He ___ here since 2020. (work)

↻ tap
A

has been working — since + duration still going → present perfect continuous.

T5

We ___ the movie last night. (enjoy)

↻ tap
A

enjoyed — finished past time (last night) → past simple (V2).

T6

At 8 p.m. yesterday, they ___ dinner. (have)

↻ tap
A

were having — in progress at a past moment → past continuous.

T7

The train ___ before we reached the station. (leave)

↻ tap
A

had left — earlier of two past events → past perfect (had + V3).

T8

She ___ for three hours when the power went off. (study)

↻ tap
A

had been studying — duration up to a past point → past perfect continuous.

T9

I think it ___ tomorrow. (rain)

↻ tap
A

will rain — prediction decided now → future simple (will + V1).

T10

This time next week, I ___ on the beach. (lie)

↻ tap
A

will be lying — in progress at a future moment → future continuous.

T11

By 2027, I ___ my degree. (complete)

↻ tap
A

will have completed — by + future deadline → future perfect.

T12

By December, she ___ here for ten years. (work)

↻ tap
A

will have been working — duration up to a future point → future perfect continuous.

Parts of Speech

P1

An interesting lecture — PoS of interesting?

↻ tap
A

Adjective — describes which kind of noun (lecture). Compare: I am interested (person = -ed).

P2

"He runs quickly." — PoS of quickly?

↻ tap
A

Adverb — answers HOW? about the verb, and -ly is the adverb stamp.

P3

"Ouch! That hurts." — PoS of Ouch!?

↻ tap
A

Interjection — sudden emotion, stands alone with an exclamation mark.

P4

"Although he studied, he failed." — type of conjunction?

↻ tap
A

Subordinating — it builds a dependent clause (not a FANBOYS coordinator).

P5

"to" in I want to sleep vs I gave it to Ali?

↻ tap
A

1st = infinitive marker (to + verb) · 2nd = preposition (to + noun phrase). Chameleon word!

P6

Name 2 FANBOYS + 2 subordinators.

↻ tap
A

FANBOYS: and, but, or, so, for, nor, yet · subordinators: because, although, if, when, while, since, until.

📝 Test Quiz — 20 MCQs (12 tenses + 8 parts of speech)

⚠️
One option per question, then Check Answers. Explanations unlock after grading. Target: 16/20 (80%)+ before you sleep. Missed one? Read the explanation, then Reset and beat your score without notes.

1She ___ in Karachi since 2019. (live)Tenses

Answer: C. since + a starting point = the action still matters now → present perfect (has/have + V3). "Lived there in 2019" would be past simple because 2019 is finished.

2Look! The baby ___. (cry)Tenses

Answer: B. Look! points to right now → present continuous (am/is/are + V-ing).

3By next year, we ___ our degree. (finish)Tenses

Answer: B. by + a future deadline = finished before that point → future perfect (will have + V3).

4He ___ his homework yesterday. (do)Tenses

Answer: B. yesterday is finished past time → past simple only. Never mix present perfect with finished-time words.

5Water ___ at 100°C. (boil)Tenses

Answer: A. Universal truths always take present simple, no matter the surrounding tense ("He said water boils at 100°C").

6I ___ TV when the lights went out. (watch)Tenses

Answer: B. Longer background action → past continuous (was watching); the interrupting event (lights went out) is past simple.

7They ___ in Dubai for five years before they moved to Lahore. (live)Tenses

Answer: C. Two past events — the earlier one (the five years) takes past perfect (had + V3); the later one (moved) stays past simple.

8This time tomorrow, we ___ on a plane. (sit)Tenses

Answer: C. "This time tomorrow" = an action in progress at that future moment → future continuous (will be + V-ing).

9I have been ill ___ Monday. (choose the correct word)Tenses

Answer: A. since + starting point (Monday) · for + duration (for five days). ❌ since 5 years is the classic mistake → ✅ for 5 years.

10Which sentence is WRONG?Tenses

Answer: A. Present perfect cannot sit inside a finished time (yesterday / ago / last week / in 2019) → fix: I saw him yesterday.

11He ___ the answer since he was ten. (know)Tenses

Answer: C. Two traps at once: know is a stative verb → never continuous ("is knowing" ❌); since → present perfect (has known). "Knew since" is also wrong.

12Did you ___ to the party? (go)Tenses

Answer: B. did already carries the past → the main verb stays in its base form (V1). ❌ "Did you went?" is the #1 beginner tense error.

13"She sings beautifully." — beautifully is an…PoS

Answer: B. It answers HOW? about the verb sings and ends in -ly → adverb. An adjective would describe a noun ("a beautiful voice").

14"The children played in the park." — in is a…PoS

Answer: B. It shows position and sits before a noun phrase ("the park") → preposition. Its PP "in the park" tells where they played.

15"I tried but failed." — but is a…PoS

Answer: C. but joins two equal clauses → coordinating conjunction (one of the FANBOYS: For And Nor But Or Yet So).

16Which word is an interjection?PoS

Answer: A. Interjections stand alone with sudden emotion + usually "!": Oh! Wow! Alas! Ouch! Therefore/however are adverbs; because is a conjunction.

17In "give me the book", me is a…PoS

Answer: B. It receives the action (after the verb) → object form. Subject would be "I": I give you the book. "My" would need a noun ("my book").

18"A happy child" — happy is a…PoS

Answer: C. It describes which kind of noun (child) and sits before it → adjective. Compare: "The child is happy" (after linking verb) and "The child smiled happily" (adverb = -ly).

19In "Light travels very fast." — which word is the noun?PoS

Answer: A. Light = the thing that travels (noun) · travels = verb · very & fast = adverbs (how much / how). Chameleon check: "a light bag" would make light an adjective.

20"Although he studied, he failed." — although is a…PoS

Answer: B. It attaches a dependent clause ("Although he studied") to the main clause. FANBOYS (and/but/or…) are the coordinating kind — they join equals.
